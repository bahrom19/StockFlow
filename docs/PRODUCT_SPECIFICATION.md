# StockFlow Enterprise Product Specification

## Version

0.1 Draft

---

# Product Overview

StockFlow Enterprise is a cloud-first ERP platform for retail businesses.

The platform combines inventory management, POS, CRM, finance, analytics and AI into one integrated system.

The system is designed for commercial SaaS deployment.

---

# Product Goals

Primary goals:

* simplify business management
* automate routine operations
* reduce inventory losses
* increase sales
* provide business analytics
* assist owners with AI recommendations
